Minor 6th

The minor 6th is the 5th of the tonic's tritone, making it a dissonant interval

Asc Examples

  • Baker street saxophone
  • In My Life (intro piano)

Desc Examples

  • Swing low, Sweet chariot (first lines)

The James bond theme (chordal intro, not the riff) is good for recognizing both the minor sixth and major sixth intervals. It starts at a fifth, then ascends to minor 6th then major, then back down.

Connections between intervals

  • The 7 of the I is the same note as the 3 of the V
    • The mental model here is that if we're playing the I chord and we know the V chord is coming up, then we can simply move back a fret from the root of the I when that V chord hits, because when it does hit, that note will be the 3 in its new context.
