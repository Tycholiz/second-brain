
## Political Thought
Democracies are based on nationalism. It is very difficult to keep a national community going unless they have strong patriotic sentiments, and feeling of connection to the nation of which they are apart

### Classical liberalism
"classical liberalism" may be described as "fiscally conservative" and "socially liberal"

Classical liberalism carries with it a central principle of individualism
- as a result, this philosophy rejects the left's inclination for collective group rights

in the United States, classical liberalism is considered closely tied to, or synonymous with, American libertarianism
