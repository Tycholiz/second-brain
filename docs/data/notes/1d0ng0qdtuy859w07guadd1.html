Job Hunting

Target engineers and hiring managers on Linkedin and ask for referrals:

  • Keep messages short and to the point: "Hey [Name], I'm really interested in the Senior Software Engineer position at [Team/Company] and l'd love to apply. I have 6 of experience in web development, and I think l'd be a great fit. Would you be open to referring me?"

Search Glassdoor, Blind, and LeetCode discussion forums for recent questions from the target company.
